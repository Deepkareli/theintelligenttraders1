The 6 Triggers

When institutions panic,
you profit.

Each trigger represents a moment when institutional risk rules force predictable, mechanical behaviour — observable in public data every single trading day.

T1
9/10 Reliability

VaR Breach — VIX Spike

When India VIX spikes 15%+ in a session, institutional risk systems auto-trigger position reduction. They must sell regardless of view. Validated: COVID 2020, Russia-Ukraine 2022.

→ Wait 30–45 min · Buy ATM calls or sell OTM puts
T2
8/10 Reliability

FII Drawdown Limit

FII net short futures above ₹15,000 Cr for 3+ days = drawdown mode. They mechanically reduce risk per internal rules. Validated: Oct 2024 record ₹1.14 lakh crore selling.

→ Do not buy calls · Wait for FII short reduction signal
T3
7/10 Reliability

Max Pain Expiry Pin

Market makers delta-hedge books on expiry creating gravitational pull toward Max Pain strike after 1:30 PM. Fires every expiry — most predictable calendar-based trigger.

→ Sell ATM straddle after 1:30 PM · Theta crushes both sides
T4
9/10 Reliability

Margin Call Cascade

All sectors falling together with no news = leveraged positions being margin-called. Not fundamental. Validated: June 2024 election crash — Nifty -5.93%, recovered in 27 days.

→ Buy calls in tranches · Recovery historically 3–27 days
T5
10/10 Reliability

Pre-Event IV Crush

Institutions buy options as insurance before RBI, Budget, Elections — then mechanically sell post-event. IV collapses 30–50% within 30 minutes regardless of outcome. Fires 8–10× per year.

→ Sell straddle 1–2 days pre-event · IV crush is mechanical
T6
7/10 Reliability

Macro FII Rule Trigger

USDINR crossing key levels or US 10Y yield spikes trigger automatic EM reallocation in global funds. India sold non-discretionarily — nothing to do with India fundamentals.

→ Watch USDINR + US 10Y · Wait for stabilisation before buying
